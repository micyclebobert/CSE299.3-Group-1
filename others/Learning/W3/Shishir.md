I learned about the  Schrödinger-picture state-vector. Apparently this is the mathematical vector in Hilbert space that carries all physical information about the quantum system at a certain time t, where the convention is that quantum state evolves over time while the operators remain unchanged. Here the operators are the functions that cause the change in the quantum system. The quantum state is said to be time dependent while the operators are time independent. An example of time independent operator are the observables such as momentum and spin which remain unchanged unless toggled externally. 

I learned about the Hamiltonian.It is a mathematical operator used in quantum mechanics that defines the total energy content of a physical system. It incorporates both kinetic and potential energy. Our system of concern here is the quantum state. It could be an individual qubit or a set of qubits. Knowing the Hamiltonian of the system lets us solve the Schrödinger equation. The solution of the Schrödinger equation tells how the quantum system evolves over time which provides us all the useful informatino that we need to know. 

I learned about the concept of time dependence and time independence in quantum mechanics. When we are dealing with a stationary quantum system whose basis remains preserved just like a qubit that has not gone through any operations, it is define by the time independent Schrödinger equation. When gate operations are done time dependence is introduced through the time dependent Schrödinger equation. Here the Hamiltonian changes as the state of the system changes. During time dependence a constant pulse of laser or any input is used that shifts the system depending on time. How is this relevant to our project? 

The gates that we apply are derived directly from the time dependent Schrödinger equation and are called time evolution operators. 

I also learned about the Z-Y-Z decomposition. Apparently the single gate operations on single qubit systems can be decomposed into parameterized rotation gates Rx Ry and Rz. The decomposition is done by Rz Ry and then Rz. There is potential for us here to improve on our system. If we are dealing with a series of single qubit operations, we can decompose them into the rotation gates and do the calculation quickly rather than performing a series of matrix multiplications. 

The universality in quantum computation. Just like there are universal classical gates, Any quantum computation can be approximated to arbitrary accuracy using only CNOT, Hadamard, and pi/8 gates. Now why are we then not just using these to do all our work. Just because they are universal does not mean they are efficient. Using one gate will have a certain degree of errors whereas using complicated set of the universal ones. For our simulation, it reduces efficiency.
