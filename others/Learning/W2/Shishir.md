I learned some theoretical stuff related to quantum information and some related to standard java script. 

The stuff on quantum information that I learned is the concept of the inner product. I learned about the bra notation alongside the ket notation. The ordinary ket notation is a representation of the quantum state in the form of a column vector. The bra notation is the representation of the same vector but in transpose and its amplitudes conjugated. I also learned that the product of the bra vector and the ket vector will give us a scalar value. The product of the bra and the ket vector is called the inner product. There are some relations to this to the magnitude of a vector also known as the Euclidean norm of a vector. Some other related concepts are conjugate symmetry and linearitiy.

I learned about Orthogonal and Orthonormal sets. If I have a set of vectors and the set is Orthogonal that means the vectors are all orthogonal to each other. Orthonormal just means the vectors are each unit vectors. Orthogonal and orthonormal basis sets are the ones where the memeber vectors form the basis of the n dimensional space they form. 

I also realized some of the common gate operations can be boiled down to direct calculation rather than going through massive matrix multiplications. This could save us compute and increase the speed of our simulator. I learned about the permutation matrix which is basically a unitary operation that only shuffles the classical states because the row and the column has only a single 1. The identity gate is one example of a permutation matrix.
